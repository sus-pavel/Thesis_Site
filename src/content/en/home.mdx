## The Research in One Paragraph

<br />
The research comprises two sequentially linked solutions, referred to as Research Propositions. The first is **audit-oriented**: electricity consumption time series are decomposed using Singular Spectrum Analysis (SSA), the components are clustered in the amplitude–frequency plane, electricity consumption patterns are reconstructed, and the **Demand Response Potential Index (DRPI)** is calculated to identify whether Demand-Side Management (DSM) is appropriate and determine preferred deployment intervals.

<br />
The second Research Proposition is **application-oriented**: electrical loads are divided into five classes, a dedicated load control model is formalized for each class, and DSM parameters are incorporated into isolated power system operating-mode optimization. The result is targeted control that accounts for technological cycles and the physical constraints of equipment.
